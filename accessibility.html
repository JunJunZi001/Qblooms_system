Accessibility Statement for EduGenie

This accessibility statement applies to the EduGenie question-generation service at chatty.inf.ed.ac.uk.

This website is run by the University of Edinburgh School of Informatics. We want as many people as possible to be able to use this service. For example, users should be able to:

  • zoom in without text becoming unreadable or controls becoming unusable;
  • navigate the main interface using a keyboard;
  • use labelled form controls to enter textbook content and generation settings;
  • read generated questions and status messages using assistive technology; and
  • understand the service using clear headings, labels and instructions.

AbilityNet provides advice on making your device easier to use . The University also explains how to customise your browser and device to improve access to web pages.

How accessible this website is

EduGenie uses labelled controls, visible keyboard focus, responsive layouts and text-based outputs. However, we know that some parts may not yet be fully accessible:

  • Some interactive components are provided by the third-party Gradio framework and have not yet been tested with a broad range of screen readers and voice-control software.
  • Dynamic progress, upload and generation updates may not always be announced consistently by every combination of browser and assistive technology.
  • AI-generated questions, answers and explanations may not always use plain language or an ideal structure for every reader.
  • User-uploaded PDF documents may be inaccessible or contain no extractable text. EduGenie cannot repair the accessibility of documents supplied by users.

Reporting accessibility problems with this website

We are always looking to improve the accessibility of this service. If you find a problem that is not listed on this page, or think that we are not meeting accessibility requirements, please report it through the Informatics Computing Help contact page or call 0131 650 4503.

Enforcement procedure

The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) is responsible for enforcing the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018. If you are not happy with how we respond to your complaint, contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS) .

You can also read the UK Government guidance on reporting an accessibility problem on a public-sector website .

Contacting us by phone or visiting us in person

The Informatics Forum is listed in the AccessAble accessibility guide . The building is wheelchair accessible and the front reception desk has an induction loop. See the Informatics Help Desk information for contact and location details.

Technical information about this website's accessibility

The University of Edinburgh is committed to making its websites accessible in accordance with the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018.

Compliance status

This website is partially compliant with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.1 AA standard because of the non-compliances and areas requiring further testing listed below.

Non-accessible content

The following issues may not meet WCAG 2.1 AA success criteria:

  • Some dynamically updated status information may not be programmatically announced in all circumstances. This may affect WCAG 2.1 success criterion 4.1.3 (Status Messages) .
  • Some third-party interactive controls still require broader keyboard and assistive technology testing. This may affect criterion 2.1.1 (Keyboard) and criterion 4.1.2 (Name, Role, Value) .

We aim to improve accessibility regularly and continuously. See “What we are doing to improve accessibility” below.

Disproportionate burden

We are not currently claiming that any accessibility problem would be a disproportionate burden to fix.

Content outside the scope of the accessibility regulations

EduGenie does not publish a library of PDF or Word documents. PDFs are uploaded temporarily by individual users as input to the service. The accessibility and structure of these user-supplied documents are outside EduGenie's control.

What we are doing to improve accessibility

  • We review the main workflow when features or interface components change.
  • We investigate and respond to accessibility issues reported by users.
  • We will test key generation and refinement workflows with additional browsers, keyboard-only navigation and screen readers.
  • We will review Gradio framework updates and adopt accessibility improvements where compatible with the service.
