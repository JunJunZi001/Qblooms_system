
1. Imagine you are a merchant in the German-speaking regions during the early 19th century. The numerous customs barriers and different currencies among the states have made your trade extremely difficult and time-consuming. Suddenly, a customs union or zollverein is formed, and the number of currencies is reduced drastically. As this merchant, how would the formation of the zollverein and the reduction in the number of currencies likely impact your business operations?

A. It would make no significant change to your business operations.
B. It would likely increase the costs and time required for your business operations.
C. It would likely decrease the costs and time required for your business operations.
D. It would probably force you to close your business due to increased competition.

Correct Answer: C

Explanation: The formation of the zollverein, or customs union, and the reduction in the number of currencies from over thirty to two would significantly streamline business operations. It would remove tariff barriers and simplify transactions, thus reducing the costs and time required for trade. This would likely lead to an increase in trade volume and profitability for merchants.
